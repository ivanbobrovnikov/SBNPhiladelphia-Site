FAQ — Window Tinting Philadelphia

What is the legal window tint percentage in Pennsylvania?

In Pennsylvania, front side windows must allow more than 70% of light in (70% VLT). Rear side windows and the rear windshield can be any darkness. We always install PA-legal tint — you'll never fail inspection because of our work.

How much does window tinting cost in Philadelphia?

Sides & rear windows on a coupe, sedan or small SUV: Standard Ceramic $199, Premium Ceramic $299, Nano Ceramic from $450. Larger SUVs and trucks are $60 more. Adding the windshield is $195 / $225 / $250 depending on film. Standard comes with a 3-year warranty; Premium and Nano come with a lifetime warranty.

What's the difference between Standard, Premium and Nano Ceramic?

All three are ceramic films that block 99% of UV and don't interfere with GPS, Bluetooth or cell signal. The difference is infrared heat rejection: Standard 60%, Premium 75%, Nano 95%+.

How long does window tinting take in Philadelphia?

Most vehicles are completed in 2–3 hours at our Philadelphia shop. We cut all film precisely to your vehicle's windows — no razor blades on the glass, clean edges every time.

Does window tint really reduce heat?

Yes — significantly. Nano ceramic tint can reject up to 99% of UV rays and dramatically reduce infrared heat buildup. On a hot Philadelphia summer day, the difference inside the car is immediate and noticeable.
