Why Northeast Philly Drivers Choose Ceramic

Northeast Philadelphia streets are tough on car paint. Salt-covered roads in winter, UV exposure in summer, and constant exposure to industrial fallout and road grime break down your clear coat faster than you might think.

Ceramic coating creates a semi-permanent nano-ceramic layer that bonds directly to your paint, providing a hydrophobic surface that repels water, UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap, and brake dust.

Unlike traditional wax that washes off in weeks, our 10-year formulation protects your investment for years with minimal maintenance.

Ceramic Coating NE Philly — FAQ

Where are you located relative to Northeast Philadelphia?

SBN Auto Styling is at 100 Red Lion Road Unit 3, Philadelphia, PA 19115 — in the heart of Northeast Philadelphia near the Bucks County border. We're accessible from I-95, Route 1, and the PA Turnpike.

What's included in the 10-year ceramic coating package?

Our 10-year package includes an exterior wash, iron decontamination, clay bar, paint enhancement, and a 10-year ceramic coating.

How long does ceramic coating take?

Most ceramic coating installations take 1–2 days. The paint decontamination and correction steps require proper curing time for best adhesion and longevity.

Can ceramic coating be applied over PPF?

Absolutely — and it's highly recommended. Ceramic coating over PPF adds hydrophobic properties that make the film easier to clean and maintain. SBN offers combined PPF + ceramic packages.

What maintenance does ceramic coating require?

Ceramic coatings are low-maintenance. Avoid automated car washes with harsh brushes. Hand washes with a pH-neutral soap every 2–4 weeks keep the coating performing at its best. We provide full aftercare instructions with every install.

Yes. We serve Mayfair, Fox Chase, Bustleton, Rhawnhurst, Somerton, Holme Circle, Torresdale, Pennypack, Roxborough, and all surrounding Northeast Philadelphia neighborhoods.
