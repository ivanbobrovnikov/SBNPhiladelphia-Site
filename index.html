What tint percentage is legal in Pennsylvania?

Front side windows must let in at least 70% of light (70% VLT). Rear side windows and the rear windshield can be any darkness. We always install PA-legal tint, so you'll never fail inspection because of our work.

What's the difference between your three tint films?

All three of our films are ceramic and block 99% of UV. The difference is infrared (heat) rejection: Standard Ceramic rejects 60%, Premium Ceramic 75%, and Nano Ceramic 95%+. None of them interfere with GPS, Bluetooth, cell signal or E-ZPass.

Is ceramic coating worth it over wax?

Wax lasts weeks. Our ceramic coating chemically bonds to your clear coat for up to 10 years, repels water, salt and bird droppings, and makes washing dramatically easier. If you're keeping your car 2+ years, it wins easily.

Do I need PPF or ceramic coating?

They solve different problems. PPF is a physical film that stops rock chips. Ceramic is a chemical layer for gloss, UV and easy cleaning. Many customers do PPF on the front end and ceramic over the whole car.

How long does each service take?

Window tint takes about 2–3 hours. Ceramic coating takes 8–10 hours. PPF takes 1–3 days depending on coverage. We'll give you an exact timeline when you book.
