What Ceramic Coating Does for Your Car

Hydrophobic Protection

Water, mud, and road grime bead off instantly. Your car stays cleaner longer and washing takes half the time.

UV & Fade Resistance

Philadelphia summers fade unprotected paint. Ceramic coating blocks UV rays at the clear coat level, keeping your color deep and rich.

Chemical Resistance

Bird droppings, tree sap, road salt — all repelled by the ceramic layer. No more etching or paint damage from common contaminants.

Deep Gloss Finish

Ceramic coating enhances the depth of your paint like nothing else. The result looks better than the day you drove it off the lot.

FAQ — Ceramic Coating Philadelphia

How much does ceramic coating cost in Philadelphia?

SBN Auto Styling's 10-year ceramic coating package is currently $499 (regularly $1,295). It includes an exterior wash, iron decontamination, clay bar, and paint enhancement — not just the coating.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Philadelphia?

Yes. Philadelphia's weather — harsh winters, road salt, summer UV — accelerates paint degradation. Ceramic coating creates a chemically bonded layer that repels water, resists UV fading, and makes washing dramatically easier. Most customers recoup the cost in reduced maintenance and better resale value.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Our 10-year package uses a high-grade formulation that bonds deeply to the clear coat for a decade of protection. It requires basic maintenance — no waxing, just regular washing.

What's included in your ceramic coating package?

The package includes an exterior wash, iron decontamination, clay bar, paint enhancement, and a 10-year ceramic coating. We never apply coating over dirty or contaminated paint.
