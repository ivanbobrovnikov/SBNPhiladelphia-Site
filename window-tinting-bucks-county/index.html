Why Choose Ceramic Tint?

Not all window film is created equal. Cheap dyed tints fade, bubble, and turn purple within a few years. Ceramic tint uses metallic oxide particles that block heat and UV without degrading over time.

Bucks County summers are intense. Ceramic tint can reduce interior cabin temperatures by 15–30°F, protecting your dashboard, seats, and leather while keeping every drive comfortable.

Unlike metalized films, ceramic and nano ceramic tints don't interfere with GPS signals, satellite radio, E-ZPass, or cell phone reception — critical for daily commuters on the PA Turnpike and I-95.

Window Tinting Bucks County — FAQ

Do you tint windows for Bucks County customers?

Yes. We serve all of Bucks County including Warminster, Langhorne, Newtown, Doylestown, Bensalem, Levittown, and surrounding areas. Our shop is at 100 Red Lion Road Unit 3, Philadelphia, PA 19115 — just a short drive from most Bucks County towns.

What are the window tint laws in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania law requires at least 70% Visible Light Transmission (VLT) on front side windows and the front windshield (above the AS-1 line). Rear side windows and the rear windshield have no VLT restriction. All our tint installations are PA-legal compliant.

What is the difference between ceramic and nano ceramic window tint?

Both block UV rays and reject heat. Nano ceramic tint uses a finer ceramic particle matrix for superior heat rejection — rejecting up to 50–60% of total solar energy versus 40–45% for standard ceramic. Nano ceramic is also signal-transparent, meaning no interference with GPS, phone signals, or toll transponders.

How long does window tinting take?

Most vehicles can be completed in 2–4 hours. We recommend avoiding rolling down windows for 3–5 days post-install to allow the adhesive to cure fully.

Does ceramic tint replace the need for sunshades?

Ceramic tint significantly reduces interior heat buildup — in some cases by 15–30°F on a hot day. While it won't completely eliminate the need for a sunshade on extreme summer days, most customers find it dramatically more comfortable and rely on sunshades far less.

How long does window tint last?

Quality ceramic and nano ceramic films last 10+ years without peeling, bubbling, or fading. Cheaper dyed films may begin to purple or bubble within 3–5 years. SBN only installs premium-grade film.
